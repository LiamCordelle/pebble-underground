# Underground

Your day as a transit map. Follow daylight, steps, heart rate and UV along colourful routes on cream paper. Tap or flick for a seven-line Service status board. Includes rolling rain forecasts, a second time zone, configurable units and step goal. Weather data by Open-Meteo. Location is used on the phone to retrieve forecasts.

Initial public version: 1.0.1.
